About

One Life Media is the creative practice of Joseph Wright, who has been creating content for over 20 years. It spans three content creation crafts — music, video and photography — driven by a fundamental desire to express: to do the impossible, and do it in an unmistakable, undeniable, groundbreaking manner. The process is intuitive and inspired rather than anything else: expression is guided in the moment, following a creative flow rooted in a direct connection to source. Joseph moves freely between the three disciplines, but always with strong focus.
