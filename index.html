From the archive

Behind the shots.

The image at the top of this page is a still from The Syndrome by Damel The Artist. We shot it at Victoria Station in rush hour, on a Sony a7 III with a Zhiyun Weebill-S gimbal.

Come My Way by Nickkle First Wav was shot entirely on a DJI Osmo Pocket, with one small Aputure light. The kit was small enough to film inside the St Pancras hotel without causing a stir. Where the light dropped, we kept the shots simple.

In November 2016 we were at the O2 Indigo in Greenwich with L.A Gray, who was supporting 112.
