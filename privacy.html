Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains how One-Life Media ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our websites. It applies to one-lifemedia.com and its sites: content.one-lifemedia.com, music.one-lifemedia.com (InBlaq Music) and photos.one-lifemedia.com (Colourzoo).

Who we are

One-Life Media is the creative practice of Joseph Wright, based in London, United Kingdom. For any privacy question, email info@one-lifemedia.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address and message. You share these when you contact us, subscribe, comment or ask about licensing.
  • Information collected automatically: your IP address, browser, device, the pages you visit and the site that sent you. Cookies and similar tools collect this for us and for the services below.

How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and provide the services or licences you request.
  • To send updates you have subscribed to (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To understand how our sites are used and improve them.
  • To show advertising that helps fund our free content.

Advertising and Google AdSense

Our sites use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to our sites and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info or www.youronlinechoices.eu.

To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Visitors in the UK, EEA and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised advertising cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time through the privacy and cookie settings link shown on our sites.

Other third-party services

Our blogs are hosted on Google Blogger and this website on GitHub Pages. Both may log technical information such as IP addresses. We may also use Google Analytics, and we embed content from YouTube, Spotify and Apple Music, which set their own cookies. Newsletter sign-ups are stored in Google Forms and Google Sheets, and our share buttons are provided by ShareThis, which sets its own cookies.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings; some features may not work correctly without them.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with the service providers described above, or where required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and subscription details only as long as we need them, or as long as the law requires.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can access, correct or delete your personal information. You can also object to or restrict its use, ask for a copy, or withdraw consent at any time. To do any of this, email info@one-lifemedia.com.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Our sites are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
