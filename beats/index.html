    How buying a beat works

    1. Listen and choose. Play the beat, then pick a WAV lease, a stems lease or an exclusive.
    2. Pay through Stripe. You'll be asked for your artist name and, if you have one, your YouTube channel. Card details go to Stripe, never to us.
    3. Get your licence. Within a minute you'll receive a licence PDF with its own number. That number is your download code: enter it with your email on the download page to get your files.
    4. Record and release. Credit the beat as "Prod. InBlaq" and send us your song title so we can register the songwriting split.

    Which licence do I need?

    WAV lease suits a first single, a mixtape track or a song you're testing with your audience. You get the full mixed beat as a WAV file and can release one song up to 100,000 streams.

    Stems lease gives you every part of the beat as a separate file (drums, bass, melodies and so on), so your engineer can mix the beat around your vocal. It covers one song up to 500,000 streams.

    Exclusive takes the beat off sale the moment you buy it, with no limit on streams. Anyone who leased it before you keeps their licence, which is standard across the industry. For a beat with many earlier leases, or a bigger project, get in touch and we'll talk it through.

    YouTube and Content ID

    If YouTube or another platform claims your song because it recognises the beat, send us your licence number and we'll clear the claim. Add your channel link at checkout and we can clear it before you upload.
