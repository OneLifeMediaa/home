Beat licence terms

Licensor: Joseph Anthony Wright, trading as InBlaq Music. Updated November 2026.

This page explains, in plain English, what you can do with a beat you license from InBlaq Music. Your licence PDF is the agreement itself; this page is the summary.

The three licences

WAV lease
Non-exclusive		WAV file of the full beat. One song. Up to 100,000 streams across all platforms combined.£30
Stems lease
Non-exclusive		WAV file plus every stem. One song. Up to 500,000 streams across all platforms combined.£75
Exclusive
Beat comes off sale		WAV file plus every stem. One song. Unlimited streams. Earlier leases stay valid.from £300

What every licence allows

  • Recording one new song over the beat.
  • Releasing that song on streaming services and for download, within your stream limit.
  • Performing the song live.
  • Making one music video for the song and putting it on your own YouTube channel and social media.

What no licence allows

  • Selling, giving away or uploading the beat on its own, without vocals.
  • Registering the beat, or your song, with YouTube Content ID or any other audio-fingerprinting service.
  • Using the beat in more than one song, or passing your licence to someone else.
  • Using the beat in film, TV, games or adverts without separate written permission.

Credit

Credit the beat as "Prod. InBlaq" in the title or description of every release and video, and in the release metadata where your distributor allows it.

Songwriting and royalties

Producing the beat makes Joseph Wright a writer of your song. He keeps 50% of the composition and you keep the other 50% for your lyrics and melody. Each of you registers your own share with your collection society, such as PRS for Music in the UK or ASCAP or BMI in the US. Please send us your song title and release date so we can register our share. You own the master recording of your song.

Going over your stream limit

Upgrade before you reach the limit. Get in touch with your licence number and you'll only pay the difference between your licence and the next one up.

Content ID claims

Some of our instrumentals are registered with Content ID through our distributor. If your song or video is claimed, send us your licence number and we'll ask for the claim to be released and your channel to be cleared.

Exclusives

When you buy an exclusive, the beat is withdrawn from sale straight away and no further licences are sold. Licences sold before your purchase stay valid until their own limits are reached. We may keep the beat in our own portfolio and on releases made before your licence date.

Checking a licence

Every licence has a unique number, such as IBQ-2026-0001. Anyone can confirm a licence is genuine on the licence check page. This helps when a distributor, playlist curator or video platform asks for proof.

Refunds

Because the files and licence are delivered straight away, licences are non-refundable once the download code has been sent, unless something has gone wrong on our side. If you've been charged twice, or an exclusive was sold to two people at once, we refund in full.

Law

These licences are governed by the law of England and Wales.
