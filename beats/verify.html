Check a licence

Every InBlaq Music beat licence has its own number, printed at the top of the licence PDF. Enter it below to confirm the licence is genuine and see what it covers.

Distributors, playlist curators and video platforms sometimes ask for proof that a beat was licensed. You can send them a link to this page with the number filled in, for example one-lifemedia.com/beats/verify.html?l=IBQ-2026-0001.
